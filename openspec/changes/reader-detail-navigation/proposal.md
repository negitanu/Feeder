## Why

The FeedAstrolabe reader retains previous/count/next controls at the end of article detail. Feeder's floating reader currently requires returning to the list to open the next article. Add these controls without reloading the list or changing browsing state.

## Correspondence

Reference: `reference/feedastrolabe/specs/reader-interactions/spec.md`, floating article detail and footer navigation. Browser URL/history navigation, keyboard arrow shortcuts, and loading unrendered pages from the footer are separate work. Legacy data import is out of scope.
