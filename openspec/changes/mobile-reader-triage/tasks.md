# Acceptance criteria

- At 320, 390 and 767px article titles can occupy two lines and selection controls appear only in selection mode.
- Swipes do not open articles or prevent vertical scroll; read, save, and star operations remain available through buttons.
- Mobile library handles appear in reorder mode and zero unread counts are hidden.
- Detail covers the viewport, keeps all six actions reachable with safe-area clearance, and restores the retained list on close.
- Verify both themes, desktop regression, keyboard accessibility, selection and synthetic touch interactions.
