## Design
Retain rendered detail only for a 160ms exit animation; make exiting content inert, aria-hidden and pointer-transparent immediately. Keep generation guards and focus restoration. Listen for outside primary pointerdown and deferred click; defer click dismissal to avoid closing a newer article opened by that same action. Full timestamps are YYYY-MM-DD HH:mm:ss JST, and unknown dates show a clear fallback. Hero renders at 480px, max-width 100%, natural ratio, hidden on failure, same-origin only. Existing close/state controls remain above the article. Shared SVG favicon is verified rather than duplicated.
