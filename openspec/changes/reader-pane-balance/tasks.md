# Acceptance criteria

- List and detail do not overlap at desktop sizes; the splitter supports pointer, keyboard, persistence and viewport changes.
- Narrow line and standard layouts show source/date above a title of up to two lines; wide and tile layouts retain the selected mode.
- Detail actions remain reachable in one sticky row, including previous/next and a safe original link; the centered column respects readability settings.
- Verify light/dark, 1024/1440/wide desktop and mobile regressions with synthetic data.
