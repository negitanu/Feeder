# Acceptance criteria

- Desktop detail and list remain visible and independently usable; Escape closes detail and preserves list state.
- Compact timestamps expose full dates; selection checkboxes appear on hover, focus, or bulk selection.
- Keyboard shortcuts operate on the selected article without intercepting typing or control activation.
- Settings links navigate to their existing sections; mobile behavior remains available.
- Verify build and desktop/mobile browser behavior with synthetic data, including both themes.
