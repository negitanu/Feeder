## ADDED Requirements
### Requirement: Independent scrolling
The desktop library sidebar SHALL remain visible below the header while the article list or settings scrolls and SHALL scroll its own overflow independently. Mobile SHALL retain independently scrolling content and library drawer without horizontal overflow.
#### Scenario: Long library and settings
- **WHEN** the reader scrolls a long sidebar or a long article list or settings page
- **THEN** the other area retains its position and remains accessible.
