## ADDED Requirements
### Requirement: Compact reader list
The reader SHALL present a compact list with readable title, source and publication time, retaining layout choices, keyboard access and mobile touch targets. The read-later filter and save controls SHALL remain available. The read-later management panel and duplicate article filter SHALL not be displayed.
#### Scenario: Read later
- **WHEN** a reader saves an article for later and chooses the read-later library view
- **THEN** the saved article appears and can be removed without a separate management panel.
